Our Classes and Subjects

OUR LEARNING STAGES

Learning at Every Stage

Our learning programmes are organised to suit students' developmental and academic needs.

01
EARLY YEARS

Nursery

Typical age range: 2–5 years

A warm introduction to learning through play, communication, discovery and guided activities.

Learning Areas

  • Early Literacy
  • Early Numeracy
  • Phonics
  • Creative Arts
  • Rhymes and Music
  • Social Skills
  • Environmental Awareness
02
FOUNDATION EDUCATION

Primary School

Typical age range: 5–11 years

Students develop strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, scientific thinking and communication.

Subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science and Technology
  • Social Studies
  • National Values
  • Computer Studies
  • Creative Arts
  • Physical and Health Education
  • Religious Studies
03
JUNIOR SECONDARY

Junior Secondary School (JSS)

Typical age range: 11–14 years

Students strengthen core knowledge, explore different subject areas and prepare for senior secondary education.

Subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Basic Science
  • Basic Technology
  • Business Studies
  • Social Studies
  • Civic Education
  • Computer Studies
  • French or Nigerian Language, where offered
  • Physical and Health Education
  • Creative and Cultural Arts
04
SENIOR SECONDARY

Senior Secondary School (SS)

Typical age range: 14–18 years

Students develop subject-specific knowledge and prepare for external examinations, further education and careers.

Core and Elective Subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Civic Education
  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • Economics
  • Government
  • Geography
  • Literature in English
  • Commerce and Accounting, where offered
  • Computer Studies, where offered
